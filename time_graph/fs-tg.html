Acknowledgments

Jemisin, N.K.. The Fifth Season. Orbit, 2015.

Research Assistant Team: Faith Lapointe and Rowan Watts

Notes: The Fifth Season does not mention any specific dates, although the chronology unfolds over the span of a few decades. The novel's storyworld may or may not be a future version of our earth. We chose dates set in the future in an effort to capture the passage of time and to allow for the possibility that the stillness is our future. The dates should not be read literally.
