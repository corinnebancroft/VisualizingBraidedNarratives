Formal structure

Overview

The formal structure infographics visualize the intertwined nature of braided narratives. Like a table of contents, these graphics show how chapters progress over the course of the novel, but they also indicate the narrator of each chapter.

How to read

Each section represents a narrative container, which we define as a narratively discrete block. These narrative blocks are often marked as separate chapters, but can be distinguished by narrative voice. For instance, in the final chapter of The History of Love, two narrators alternate every page or couple of pages. The height of the section, measured along the interior zigzag, is determined by the length of the chapter and the colour corresponds to the narrator. These infographics indicate that narrators in a braided narrative alternate in a pattern, so braided narratives are more interconnected on this level than non-braided narratives.
