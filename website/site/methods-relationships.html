Relationships Method

Directional: We record direction of exchanges and relationships. This means there is a source (the person speaking) and a target (the person listening). Character 1 is the source and Character 2 is the target. If the person listening responds, then it is a reciprocal exchange. The same thing goes for relationships. If the source sees the target, it is a one way relationship. If the target sees the source as well, it is a reciprocal relationship. Stewart has added a “is reciprocal box.” If the relationship is reciprocal, select true. If it is not reciprocal, select false.

Exchanges: All exchanges are relationships. If there is an exchange between two characters on a page, you do not need to record a relationship. All exchanges are reciprocal (one character speaks/ writes/ communicates and another receives that communication even if they don’t respond) unless otherwise indicated by situations like the following:

  • A letter is intercepted and doesn’t reach its recipient
  • A TV news anchor communicates the news, but the viewers cannot respond
  • Eavesdropping is a one-way exchange between the person speaking and the person hearing

Relationships: Instead of MENTIONS, let’s do RELATIONSHIPS:

Relationships are:

  • Any declaration of a relationship
    • Mr and Mrs Dursley live at Privet drive declares that the two have a relationship. (Mutual relationship)
    • Harry Potter, Lily and James's son declares another relationship (Mutual relationship)
    • After establishing a relationship, you do not need to record it again unless the relationship is explicitly named:
      • Did Voldemort kill Harry, the Potter's son? (One way relationship between Voldemort and Harry; but also Declares a mutual relationship between Harry and his parents)
      • Did Voldemort kill Harry Potter? (One way relationship between Voldemort and Harry Potter; The relationship between Harry and his parents is implied but not explicitly named)
  • Physical interaction between characters
    • Voldemort tried to kill Harry Potter (One way relationship)
    • Mr Dursley pecked Mrs Dursley on the cheek (One way relationship)
  • One character referring to another character
    • Professor McGonagall said Harry Potter survived. (One way relationship between McGonagall and HP)
  • Embedded references refer to multiple relationships
    • Professor McGonagall said Hagrid said Harry Potter survived. (One way relationship between McGonagall and Hagrid, one way relationship between Hagrid and HP, one way relationship between McGonagall and Harry Potter)
  • Perception and thought counts as a relationship
    • Mr Dursley saw the cat (One way relationship between Mr Dursley and McGonagall)
