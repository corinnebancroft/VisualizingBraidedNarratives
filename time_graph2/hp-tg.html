  • Event: Harry's 11th Birthday

    Date: July 31, 1990

    Narrator: 3rd Person

    Participating Characters:

    Evidence: Harry thinks “then tomorrow, Tuesday, was Harry’s eleventh birthday" which led to the understanding that the year was 1990. However, several sources argue that Harry's 11th birthday took place in 1991 which is solidified by later texts. For the purpose of this timegraph we have chosen to use the 1990 calendar. His birthday is also the day Voldemort/Quirrell breaks into Gringotts.
